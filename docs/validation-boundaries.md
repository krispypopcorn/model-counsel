# Validation Boundaries

Model Counsel applies deterministic structural checks to nondeterministic model output. It can
detect missing files, truncated reports, absent sections, insufficient source links, malformed
shortlists, duplicate shortlist candidates, missing completion markers, and candidate reports
that do not name their assigned opportunity.

These checks do not establish source quality, confirm that a URL supports a claim, verify a
contract value, or make a commercial recommendation safe. The final artifacts are decision
support for human review, not autonomous approval to bid, spend, purchase inventory, extend
credit, or sign a contract.
